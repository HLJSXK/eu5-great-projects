# Unique Wonder Rituals

Unique wonders use the unified system in [`wonder_ceremony.md`](wonder_ceremony.md).
They inherit all three reward packages, societal directions and estate preferences
through `mechanic_key`; only their three bilingual names/descriptions are authored
in `ritual_text`.

Pharos and Hagia Sophia use this same flow. Do not reintroduce bespoke route or
assignment systems, eight-stage ceremonies, the design harness, or design-browser
schemas. Historical versions remain in Git history.
